Marketing Execution Plan

The Post-Practice Tradition

Duration 8 weeks (compressible to 6) Goal Convert the sports-park audience already passing the door Prepared for The owner of Simbongsa

1. The one-line strategy

Simbongsa isn't competing with other Korean restaurants — it's competing with families driving straight home after practice. The audience is already nearby, already hungry, and already has a reason to stop. The job is conversion, not awareness.

Everything below is built to turn "we drove past it" into "we always stop there after practice."

The three offers — the engine

OfferWhat it isJob it does
Practice Day SpecialA small bonus item for guests in a sports uniform / practice attireBuilds the habit and rewards the exact moment we want to own
Family BundlesFamily-sized combinations priced as a setLifts average order value
First-Time FavoritesA "not sure what to get?" starter guide at the counter + onlineRemoves hesitation → converts first-timers

The three tracking codes — how we prove it worked

STORYPRACTICECREATOR

Every offer and post carries one, so each real visit ties back to the marketing that drove it. Likes don't equal sales; redeemed codes do.

2. Success metrics

MetricTarget by end of campaign
Peak-hour (post-practice) transactions+15%
Average order value+10%
Repeat visits+15%
Marketing attributionEvery campaign traceable to redeemed codes

Leading indicators (watch weekly): code redemptions, bundle attach rate, % of transactions in the 4–7pm post-practice window, new-vs-returning mix, content saves/shares (not just likes).

3. Roles & tools

  • Owner / manager: approves offers, briefs staff, reviews the weekly numbers.
  • Counter staff: apply the Practice Day bonus, record the code at checkout, hand out First-Time Favorites cards.
  • Content lead: shoots and posts content, runs the calendar, compiles the weekly report.
  • Tools: POS with a coupon button per code (or a tally sheet), Instagram, a printed First-Time Favorites card, one shared log spreadsheet.
Zero-budget path: phone camera + Instagram + tally sheet + printed cards. Paid boosts are optional and only introduced once we know which posts convert (Week 5+).

4. Week 0 — Setup (the 3–5 days before launch)

Do not launch until these are done. This week is what makes the results measurable.

  • Finalize the Practice Day Special bonus item (low-cost, high-perceived-value).
  • Define the 3 launch Family Bundles with set pricing and names.
  • Write and print the First-Time Favorites card: "New here? Start with these 3." Pin it on Instagram too.
  • Set up tracking codes in the POS (or tally sheet): STORY, PRACTICE, CREATOR.
  • Create the weekly log spreadsheet (date, transactions, post-practice count, redemptions by code, bundles sold, new/returning).
  • Brief counter staff on applying the bonus and logging the code.
  • Shoot a batch of 5–7 content pieces so Week 1 isn't scrambling.
  • Record a baseline: current peak-hour transactions, AOV, and repeat rate.

5. The content system (runs all 8 weeks)

Cadence: 4–5 posts/week + daily stories. Post the conversion content on practice days, 1–2 hours before pickup/dinner.

Recurring formats (rotate)

  • 🏆 Best Post-Practice Order — the go-to after a game.
  • 🧒 Kid Favorite of the Week — kids drive the snack decision.
  • 📖 First-Time Customer Guide — what to order if you've never had Korean street food.
  • 🎽 Practice Day Special — reminder of today's bonus, with the PRACTICE code.
Rule: every post ends with a reason to walk in — a code, a time, or a "stop by after practice today" — not just a reason to like.

6. Week-by-week execution

Weeks 1–2

Launch the Practice Day Special

Focus: Start the habit; get the measurement loop running.

  • Launch the bonus for guests in practice attire; announce with the PRACTICE code.
  • Post Practice Day Special + Best Post-Practice Order formats heavily.
  • Staff log every code redemption from day one.
  • Checkpoint (end of Wk 2): Are codes being redeemed and logged? Fix the loop before adding complexity.

KPI focus: code redemptions · post-practice-window transaction count.

Weeks 3–4

Launch Family Bundles

Focus: Lift average order value.

  • Introduce the 3 bundles; feature them in content and at the counter.
  • Content shifts to bundle storytelling + Kid Favorite of the Week.
  • Train staff to offer the bundle at the point of sale.

KPI focus: bundle attach rate · average order value vs baseline.

Weeks 5–6

Expand what's working

Focus: Double down; stop guessing.

  • Pull the numbers: which posts drove redemptions? Which bundle sells? Which code performs?
  • Reallocate effort to the top formats; retire what isn't converting.
  • Deploy STORY / CREATOR codes deliberately (e.g. a local sports-parent creator collab or a story-only offer).
  • Optional first small paid boost behind the single best post.

KPI focus: cost-per-redemption by channel · repeat-visit signals.

Weeks 7–8

Optimize on data

Focus: Lock in the tradition; decide what continues.

  • Refine offers, timing, and bundle mix using 6 weeks of tracked results.
  • Formalize the winning weekly rhythm as the post-campaign "new normal."
  • Build the wrap report: lift vs baseline on all four metrics, best content, best offer, keep/kill list.

KPI focus: the four headline targets (+15% peak-hour, +10% AOV, +15% repeat, attribution live).

7. The weekly rhythm

  1. Mon: Plan the week's posts around practice/game days; prep any offer.
  2. Tue–Sat: Post on cadence; staff log codes at checkout.
  3. Sun (15 min): Update the log, review leading indicators, note one thing to change.
The loop is the whole system: post → offer → redeem → log → adjust.

8. Compressing to 6 weeks

  • Weeks 1–2: Practice Day Special + Family Bundles together (heavier Week 0 prep).
  • Weeks 3–4: Expand best content + introduce STORY / CREATOR.
  • Weeks 5–6: Optimize on data + wrap report.

Trade-off: less time to clean up the measurement loop before adding the bundle — so Week 0 setup and staff briefing matter even more.

9. Risks & adjustments

RiskSignalAdjustment
Codes not being loggedRedemptions implausibly lowRe-brief staff; simplify to one tally sheet
Bonus item too costlyMargin dipsSwap to a lower-cost bonus of equal perceived value
Likes, not visitsHigh views, flat redemptionsRewrite CTAs to time + code; post closer to pickup
One offer outperformsSkewed redemptionsReallocate — don't keep funding the loser for "balance"

Bottom line

Simbongsa already has the location, the customers, and the product. This plan adds the missing piece: a repeatable, measurable reason for families to stop after every practice — and the tracking to prove it works.
