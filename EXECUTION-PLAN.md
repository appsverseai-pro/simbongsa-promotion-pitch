# Simbongsa — Marketing Execution Plan

**Campaign:** The Post-Practice Tradition
**Duration:** 8 weeks (compressible to 6 — see note)
**Goal:** Convert the sports-park audience that already passes the door into repeat, measurable customers.
**Prepared for:** The owner of Simbongsa · 심봉사도로께

---

## 1. The one-line strategy

Simbongsa isn't competing with other Korean restaurants — it's competing with families driving **straight home** after practice. The audience is already nearby, already hungry, and already has a reason to stop. **The job is conversion, not awareness.**

Everything below is built to turn "we drove past it" into "we always stop there after practice."

### The three offers (the engine)

| Offer | What it is | Job it does |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Practice Day Special** | A small bonus item for guests in a sports uniform / practice attire | Builds the *habit* and rewards the exact moment we want to own |
| **Family Bundles** | Family-sized combinations priced as a set | Lifts **average order value** |
| **First-Time Favorites** | A "not sure what to get?" starter guide at the counter + online | Removes hesitation → converts first-timers |

### The three tracking codes (how we prove it worked)

`STORY` · `PRACTICE` · `CREATOR` — every offer and post carries one, so each real visit ties back to the marketing that drove it. **Likes don't equal sales; redeemed codes do.**

---

## 2. Success metrics (what we're driving toward)

| Metric | Target by end of campaign |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Peak-hour (post-practice) transactions | **+15%** |
| Average order value | **+10%** |
| Repeat visits | **+15%** |
| Marketing attribution | Every campaign traceable to redeemed codes |

**Leading indicators to watch weekly:** code redemptions, bundle attach rate, % of transactions in the 4–7pm post-practice window, new-vs-returning mix, content saves/shares (not just likes).

---

## 3. Roles & tools (keep it lightweight)

- **Owner / manager:** approves offers, briefs counter staff, reviews the weekly numbers.
- **Counter staff:** apply the Practice Day bonus, ask for/record the code at checkout, hand out First-Time Favorites cards.
- **Content lead (you / the marketer):** shoots and posts content, runs the calendar, compiles the weekly report.
- **Tools:** POS with a discount/coupon button per code (or a simple tally sheet if the POS can't), Instagram, a printed First-Time Favorites card, one shared spreadsheet for the weekly log.

> **Zero-budget path:** phone camera + Instagram + a tally sheet + printed cards. Paid boosts are optional and only introduced once we know which posts convert (Week 5+).

---

## 4. Week 0 — Setup (the 3–5 days before launch)

Do not launch until these are done. This week is what makes the results *measurable*.

- [ ] Finalize the **Practice Day Special** bonus item (low-cost, high-perceived-value — e.g. a mini korokke or a small drink).
- [ ] Define the **3 launch Family Bundles** with set pricing and names (e.g. "Team Snack Box," "Family Four," "Practice Night Combo").
- [ ] Write and print the **First-Time Favorites** card: "New here? Start with these 3." Place at the counter + save as a pinned IG post/highlight.
- [ ] Set up **tracking codes** in the POS (or tally sheet): `STORY`, `PRACTICE`, `CREATOR`.
- [ ] Create the **weekly log spreadsheet** (columns: date, transactions, post-practice-window count, code redemptions by code, bundles sold, new/returning).
- [ ] **Brief counter staff:** how to apply the bonus, how to ask "did anything bring you in today?" and log the code.
- [ ] Shoot a **batch of content** (5–7 pieces) so Week 1 isn't scrambling.
- [ ] Baseline snapshot: record current peak-hour transactions, AOV, and repeat rate so we can measure lift.

---

## 5. The content system (runs all 8 weeks)

**Cadence:** 4–5 posts/week + daily stories. Post the conversion-driving content **on practice days, timed 1–2 hours before pickup/dinner** ("here's why you stop by after practice today").

**Recurring formats (rotate these):**
- 🏆 **Best Post-Practice Order** — the go-to after a game.
- 🧒 **Kid Favorite of the Week** — kids drive the snack decision.
- 📖 **First-Time Customer Guide** — what to order if you've never had Korean street food.
- 🎽 **Practice Day Special** — reminder of today's bonus, with the `PRACTICE` code.

**Rule:** every post ends with a reason to *walk in*, not just to like — a code, a time, or a "stop by after practice today."

---

## 6. Week-by-week execution

### Weeks 1–2 · Launch the Practice Day Special
**Focus:** Start the habit; get the measurement loop running.
- Launch the bonus item for guests in practice attire; announce with the `PRACTICE` code.
- Post the Practice Day Special + Best Post-Practice Order formats heavily.
- Counter staff log every code redemption from day one.
- **First checkpoint (end of Week 2):** Are codes being redeemed? Is staff logging consistently? Fix the loop before adding complexity.
- **KPI focus:** code redemptions, post-practice-window transaction count.

### Weeks 3–4 · Launch Family Bundles
**Focus:** Lift average order value.
- Introduce the 3 bundles; feature them in content and on the counter/menu.
- Content shifts to bundle storytelling ("feed the whole team," "the car-ride-home box") + Kid Favorite of the Week.
- Train staff to *offer the bundle* at the point of sale ("make it a Family Four?").
- **KPI focus:** bundle attach rate, average order value vs baseline.

### Weeks 5–6 · Expand what's working
**Focus:** Double down; stop guessing.
- Pull the numbers: which posts drove redemptions? Which bundle sells? Which code performs?
- Reallocate content effort to the top formats. Retire what isn't converting.
- Introduce the `STORY` and `CREATOR` codes more deliberately — e.g. a local sports-parent creator/collab, or a story-only offer — to test new conversion sources.
- Optional first **small paid boost** behind the single best-performing post.
- **KPI focus:** cost-per-redemption by channel, repeat-visit signals (returning faces/codes).

### Weeks 7–8 · Optimize on data
**Focus:** Lock in the tradition; decide what continues.
- Refine offers, timing, and bundle mix using 6 weeks of tracked results.
- Formalize the winning weekly rhythm as the "new normal" Simbongsa runs after the campaign.
- Build the **campaign wrap report**: lift vs baseline on the four success metrics, best content, best offer, recommended keep/kill list.
- **KPI focus:** the four headline targets (+15% peak-hour, +10% AOV, +15% repeat, attribution live).

---

## 7. The weekly rhythm (every week, same loop)

1. **Mon:** Plan the week's posts around practice/game days. Prep any offer.
2. **Tue–Sat:** Post on cadence; staff log codes at checkout.
3. **Sun (15 min):** Update the log, eyeball leading indicators, note one thing to change next week.

This loop is the whole system: **post → offer → redeem → log → adjust.**

---

## 8. Compressing to 6 weeks

If a 6-week runway is preferred, merge the phases:
- **Weeks 1–2:** Practice Day Special **+** Family Bundles together (heavier Week 0 prep required).
- **Weeks 3–4:** Expand best-performing content + introduce `STORY`/`CREATOR`.
- **Weeks 5–6:** Optimize on data + wrap report.

Trade-off: less time to clean up the measurement loop before adding the bundle, so Week 0 setup and staff briefing matter even more.

---

## 9. Risks & how we adjust

| Risk | Signal | Adjustment |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Codes not being logged | Redemptions look implausibly low | Re-brief staff; simplify to a single tally sheet |
| Bonus item too costly | Margin dips | Swap to a lower-cost bonus with equal perceived value |
| Content gets likes, not visits | High views, flat redemptions | Rewrite CTAs to time + code; post closer to pickup |
| One offer outperforms | Skewed redemptions | Reallocate — don't keep funding the loser for "balance" |

---

## 10. Bottom line

Simbongsa already has the **location**, the **customers**, and the **product**. This plan adds the missing piece: a repeatable, measurable reason for families to stop **after every practice** — and the tracking to prove it's working.

**Practice → 심봉사도로께 → Home.**
